Quick Reference

Rules & tips — concise

Short Rest

A short rest is at least 1 hour. Spend Hit Dice to regain HP: roll each Hit Die and add your CON modifier to each die roll.

Long Rest

A long rest is 8 hours. You regain all HP, and recover spent Hit Dice up to half your total (rounded up).

Concentration

When you take damage while concentrating, you must make a CON save with DC = max(10, damage/2). On fail, you lose concentration.

Combat Basics

Advantage: roll 2d20 take higher. Disadvantage: roll 2d20 take lower.
